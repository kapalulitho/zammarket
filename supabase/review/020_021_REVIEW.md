## 020 Specialist catalogues

New tables: specialist_settings (allow_publishing FALSE), specialist_listings (agriculture/digital, public catalogue fields, owner, price/unit/quantity/licence, media paths, draft/published/closed, timestamps), digital_private_assets (listing/owner/path/version, pending/approved/blocked scan status), digital_entitlements (asset, buyer, unique purchase reference, issue/revocation times). Full columns/types/checks are in 020_specialist_catalogues_HOLD_DRAFT.sql.

RLS on all four tables. Listing draft reads/writes require owner identity and verified completed membership; identity/kind immutable. Publication gate defaults FALSE and public rows disappear when disabled. Preview image paths must already exist in the owner's storage folder. No client delete/ownership/scan/entitlement mutation grants. Assets are owner-readable, entitlements buyer-readable; private files and purchase references are never projected through catalogue rows. Trusted service grants are narrowed after default-grant revocation.

Two new PRIVATE buckets only: specialist-previews (JPG/PNG/WebP, 5 MB) and digital-products (PDF/ZIP, 25 MB). Restrictive policies protect against other permissive Storage rules, prohibit ordinary-client overwrite/delete and enforce own-folder uploads. Published preview access follows published references and the publication flag. No legacy bucket settings/policies are replaced.

Functions/triggers: specialist_publication_enabled, specialist_listing_guard (insert/update trigger), digital_asset_guard (insert trigger), specialist_media_visible, digital_asset_access, digital_download_eligible. All functions use empty search_path and explicit EXECUTE grants; triggers cannot be called as RPCs. Ownership guard validates digital listing and existing own-path object. Client insert grants exclude scan_status. Service update grants are scan-status-only; file path/owner/version identity cannot be switched through ordinary update privileges.

Buyers **do not receive Storage SELECT rights**, even after entitlement. digital_download_eligible returns only their eligibility boolean after a scan-approved asset and active entitlement. A future trusted download endpoint must recheck purchaser identity, entitlement and scan status, locate the asset server-side and mint a tightly bounded short-lived URL. This prevents purchasers using the Storage SDK to mint arbitrary-duration URLs themselves. There is no download endpoint or actual purchase/entitlement issuer in this batch. Already issued bearer URLs can be shared until expiry and copied files cannot be recalled; this design is not DRM.

**Remaining blockers:** permitted-goods/licence moderation, malware scanning/quarantine workflow, audited genuine purchase proof, immutable issuance/revocation process, endpoint authentication/rate limits/TTL, native Storage validation and retention policy. A purchase_reference is an authority-supplied reference, not independent proof of a transaction. No real payment or file delivery may be activated merely because these tables exist. Frontend catalogues remain in memory and are not wired to this schema; an approved adapter must map frontend kind/licence field names.

## 021 Discovery activity / trends

New settings (publish_trends FALSE), curated topics, private discovery_activity and thresholded discovery_rollups. The ledger stores topic IDs, server-generated pseudonymous actor hash, action, database-assigned hourly bucket/time; **no raw search text, login email, IP address or exact location**. Labels/area names are curated public topics, not submitted user query strings.

No client can ingest events or read activity. Trusted-service insert is guarded by actor lock, eight-events/minute limit, DB timestamps and unique topic/actor/action/hour deduplication. Rates alone do not prove a real person: the future collector must derive HMAC actor tokens itself, validate consent/identity, refuse client-selected actor hashes and detect coordinated/Sybil abuse. Rotate/retain tokens only under an approved privacy plan. No collector, consent service, aggregation job, scheduled deletion or telemetry transmission is enabled.

Public topics/rollups have RLS and require the publication flag, active topic and minimum five distinct actors. Scores/windows are bounded. discovery_trends is an explicit security_invoker view exposing curated kind/label/location, time window and score only, never actor hashes/ledger rows. Rollups are service-written; a trusted audited aggregation pipeline must compute them from genuine signals and independently verify actor thresholds/time windows. Do not seed synthetic aggregates as real popularity data. Thresholding reduces disclosure but is not a proof of anonymity.

Frontend Trending Now explains that community data is absent. Optional visit-only interest ranking uses real local clicks, confirmed saves and Enter-submitted searches, with memory-only consent, deduplication, rate/cap limits, time decay and location filtering. It is clearly labelled personal interest rather than a community trend and never reorders homepage service icons. No browser events are transmitted to 021.

## Safety / verification / recommendation

Local disposable checks passed: publication rejection, owner/private/quarantine boundaries, no forged scans or purchase entitlements, scan/revocation-aware eligibility, no buyer Storage URL minting, private/deduplicated activity, rollup threshold and publication gate. Old policy definitions remained intact. These tests do not validate real Storage HTTP, scanner execution, paid-purchase proof, signing endpoint or multi-session abuse prevention.
